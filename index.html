How it works

Different targets.
Same playbook.

01

Find an enemy.

Identify a group that can be made responsible for something people fear, resent or believe they have lost.

02

Feed the grievance.

Repeat the message until a complicated problem has a simple explanation and somebody to blame.

03

Offer the solution.

Present Reform as the only force willing to confront the people supposedly responsible.

This site documents the people and institutions Reform UK identifies as the problem — using Reform's own words wherever possible.

The question isn't simply what Reform says. It is what happens when the same political technique is applied, again and again, to different groups.
