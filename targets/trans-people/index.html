The playbook

Turn people
into an idea.

Look at the words Reform uses.

Ideology. Indoctrination. Poison. Chaos.

None of them describes a person.

That is the point. You can't ban a person from existing. But you can promise to ban an ideology.

So being trans is recast as something that is taught, spread and caught, like a doctrine pushed on children. Trans people themselves disappear from the argument: the trans adults who have lived quietly for decades, the trans teachers, the trans parents.

Then comes the threat: “irreversible harm to children”.

And then the offer: Reform will ban it.

But the people behind the “ideology” are real, and they live here. Here is what the evidence says about them.

The reality

Who's really
under threat?

0.5%

of adults in England and Wales said their gender identity was different from their sex registered at birth. That's about 262,000 people, in the 2021 Census.

The ONS classes these figures as official statistics in development and advises that they carry more uncertainty than most census results.

6×

Trans people were around six times as likely as other adults to be victims of personal hate crime: 1.3% compared with 0.2%.

Crime Survey for England and Wales, April 2022 to March 2025.

3,809

transgender hate crimes were recorded by police in England and Wales in the year to March 2025. The figure excludes the Metropolitan Police. These crimes were less likely to end in a charge than comparable crimes with no hate element.

The Home Office warns that police-recorded figures are not a reliable guide to trends over time.

2004

Parliament passed the Gender Recognition Act, giving trans people legal recognition. Protection from discrimination because of gender reassignment dates back to 1999.

Being trans isn't a doctrine invented in classrooms. Trans people have been recognised in UK law for more than twenty years.

In April 2025, the Supreme Court ruled that “sex” in the Equality Act means biological sex. It also said the ruling does not remove trans people's protection from discrimination.

Trans people are a small minority. The official figures show that they are far more likely than other adults to be targeted by hate crime.

An ideology can't be the victim of a hate crime.

People can.
